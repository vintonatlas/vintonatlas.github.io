Say It Right

Tap a name to hear it. Your phone's own voice reads it in the right language; the respelling shows how to say it as an American speaker.

Capital letters mark the stressed syllable: bor-DOH sounds like "bore-DOE". The number is the atlas page.

This browser cannot speak aloud, so the play buttons are switched off. Every respelling still works: read the capital letters a little louder.

Where the voices come from. Nothing is recorded here: your phone, tablet or computer reads each name with the voices it already has, choosing a French voice for French names, an Italian voice for Italian names, and so on. If your device has no voice for a language (Georgian is rare), an American English voice reads the book's respelling instead. Voices differ from device to device, and so do native speakers; the respelling is always your safe guide.

Want more voices? On iPhone and iPad go to Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices, pick a language and download a voice (the Enhanced ones sound best). On Android, look in Settings for Text-to-speech output and install extra language data. On a Mac, System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > System Voice > Manage Voices.
